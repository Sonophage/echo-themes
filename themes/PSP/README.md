# PSP

The crossbar as it first looked on a handheld: a deep blue gradient, the wave drifting across it, and
thin white icons with a soft glow.

- **Icons**: every crossbar, menu and status icon, and a label for every console, drawn as white line art.
- **Wallpaper**: the blue gradient; ECHO draws the PSP wave over it.
- **Sounds**: 8 short interface sounds.
- **theme.json**: PSP wave, Disc for GameBoot and launch, PlayStation buttons.

Original art, made for ECHO in the spirit of the original menu. No Sony artwork or logos are included.
